Multi-agent research pipeline

AgentOS

You give it an open-ended research question and it hands back a structured Markdown report with citations. Underneath is a LangGraph state graph with five stages: a planner, web search, retrieval, a code executor and a critic.

The critic is the part I care about most. It reviews the draft and can send it back for another pass, with a cap so it can't loop forever. Progress streams to the client over server-sent events from FastAPI, Qdrant keeps memory of earlier runs, and Redis handles caching.

  • LangGraph
  • FastAPI
  • Qdrant
  • Redis
  • SSE

Retrieval

Hybrid RAG

Dense search is good at meaning and bad at exact terms. BM25 is the opposite. This pipeline runs both, BGE embeddings in Qdrant alongside BM25, and merges the two rankings with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, so the scores never have to share a scale.

A BGE cross-encoder reranks the shortlist before anything reaches the model. Each answer then goes through a citation check, and RAGAS scores the pipeline so I'm not judging it by how fluent the output sounds.

  • Qdrant
  • BGE
  • BM25
  • RRF
  • RAGAS

Applied ML prototype

Healthcare Analytics

A prototype, and I'd rather call it that than oversell it. It covers two tracks: image classification for MRI and chest X-rays, and prediction models for patient risk, length of stay and readmission. The interface shows why a model said what it said, not only the result.

React on the front, FastAPI and PyTorch behind it, MLflow and DVC for tracking experiments and data. None of it has been clinically validated.

Agentic fitness and nutrition tracker

The Cut

A nutrition, training and transformation tracker. An AI planner builds your first plan and training week from your profile, always as a draft you choose to apply.

The coach always works from the live plan: database rules turn your logs into real-time insights, it reviews your own plan edits, and in chat it uses tools but can only propose drafts, never change your plan.
