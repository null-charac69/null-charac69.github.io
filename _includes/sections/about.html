01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE SYSTEMS

Good AI engineering is
more than a good prompt.

I work at the point where model capability meets software engineering. My focus is turning promising AI ideas into systems that can be understood, evaluated and operated.

My work centres on Generative AI and LLM-based applications — especially retrieval, orchestration and the services that make them useful in real workflows. I move between experimentation and implementation, paying attention to the details that decide whether a prototype can become a dependable product.

I started out in Mechanical Engineering at NIT Srinagar. That background still shapes how I approach software: understand the constraints, reason about the whole system, and validate the result rather than relying on assumptions.
