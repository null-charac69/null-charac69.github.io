About

Mechanical engineer by degree, AI engineer by trade.

Most of what I do is LLM applications: retrieval, orchestration, and the services between a model and the people using it.

Calling the model is rarely the hard part. The time goes into getting the right context in front of it, checking that the answer is supported by something, and keeping the whole thing fast and cheap enough to run. That's the work I like.

I studied mechanical engineering at NIT Srinagar before moving into AI. Two habits came with me: work out the constraints before designing anything, and test instead of assuming.
